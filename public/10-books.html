10 Books Every Single Person Who's Tired of Waiting Should Read Before Another Year Passes

One of these was written for exactly what you're going through.

Jump to your book  ↓

You've been to the weddings. You've clapped, you've prayed, you've smiled when someone asked, "So, when is yours?" And on the drive home, you wondered quietly whether something is wrong with you.

Nothing is wrong with you. But something is getting in the way — and until you find out what it is, another year will look exactly like this one.

These 10 books were written for people in your exact situation: single, sincere, and tired of waiting without a plan. Some are for women who feel the clock. Some are for men who can't seem to settle down. Read through them, find the one that sounds like your own thoughts, and download it before you leave this page.

Which one should you start with?
